About Us

Welcome to Best Cars Dealership, home to the best cars in North America. We deal in selling domestic and imported cars at reasonable prices.

Our Story

Founded in 1998 by automotive enthusiast James Harrison, Best Cars Dealership began as a modest family-owned lot with just 15 vehicles and a simple mission: to make quality transportation accessible to everyone. What started as a weekend passion project in a small Midwestern town has grown into one of the region's most trusted dealerships, with three locations serving thousands of satisfied customers annually.

Over the past 25 years, we've weathered economic cycles, embraced technological change, and expanded our inventory to include both domestic and imported vehicles—all while staying true to our founding principle: the customer comes first. Every car on our lot undergoes a rigorous 150-point inspection before it ever reaches our showroom floor.
